claim to incorporate errata into immutable RFC text.

## Selected SEVOCAB definitions

The [public catalogue](https://sevocab.computer.org/sev_display/index.action)
permits copying ISO/IEC definitions with source citation and IEEE definitions
with the specified notice. Each selected JSON file preserves source attribution
and the notice `Copyright©, 2026, IEEE. Used by permission.` The year follows the
notice at retrieval, rather than being inferred from a source standard's edition.
ISO/IEC attribution is also retained per entry.

Only the first attributable ISO/IEEE definition selected by the documented policy
is captured for each of eight exact terms. PMI/PMBOK definitions are excluded.
Multiple definitions can exist for the same term; this selection does not erase
the alternatives or prescribe the right definition for every project.

Visible text is extracted with whitespace normalization. This is a quotation
collection, not a byte-for-byte HTML mirror, full vocabulary export, ISO PDF or
official translation. Session identifiers and unrelated page content are excluded.
The complete standard remains available from its publisher under its own terms.
